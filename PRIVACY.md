# Privacy Policy for Lynia

Last updated: June 13, 2026

This privacy policy explains how your information is collected, used, and disclosed by the Lynia Discord bot.

## 1. Information We Collect
To function correctly, the Lynia bot may process the following data:
- **Discord User ID:** To identify users and manage permissions or settings.
- **Server ID:** To associate data with specific Discord servers where the bot is used.
- **Command Usage:** We may log command usage for debugging and service improvement.

## 2. How We Use Information
We use the collected information only for:
- Providing the core features of the bot.
- Troubleshooting and improving bot performance.
- Ensuring compliance with Discord's Terms of Service.

## 3. Data Protection
We take reasonable measures to protect your information. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

## 4. Contact
If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact the developer via Discord or the support server.
